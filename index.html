Filming tips
  • Film from the side, camera at hip height, level, and still (tripod or treadmill).
  • Keep the whole body in frame, including the feet. Good light helps the pose model.
  • Higher frame rates capture the exact moment of foot strike; 120 fps or more is ideal.
  • Phone slow-mo: if the file plays back slowed down (e.g. shared/exported from Photos), set Footage speed below so timing metrics are in real time.
